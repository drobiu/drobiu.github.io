## Bio

Karol Dobiczek is a PhD Researcher in the Department of Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence at Jagiellonian University. He holds both his BSc (2022) and MSc (2024) in Computer Science from Delft University of Technology.

His research focuses on explainability and validation of AI models in expert domains. His work includes the analysis of the interplay of modalities in Earth observation and medical tasks, validating foundation models in medicine and NLP. 

From October 2026 through December 2026 Karol is a Visiting Researcher at ESA Φ-lab. His project focuses on explainability for multimodal GeoFoundational models, characterizing their inner workings through explanations and analyzing their data representations over varying data sources and tasks in remote sensing.
